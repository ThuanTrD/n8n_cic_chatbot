# Preliminary Definition of Done

Success means a trusted Drive file can be deterministically listed, validated, OCRed, versioned, chunked, embedded and finalized in a product-independent legal corpus, while unchanged files are skipped and partial failures remain non-current.

Forbidden outcomes: product coupling, invented legal metadata, cross-organization access, duplicate current versions, retrieval of partial versions, unbounded file/model work, deletion from Drive synchronization, workflow activation, live customer messages, or secrets/raw documents in Git.
