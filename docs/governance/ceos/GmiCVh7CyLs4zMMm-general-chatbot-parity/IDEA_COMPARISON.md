# Idea Comparison

## A. Clone the IntelliCAD workflow
Fast but unsafe: duplicates two product-specific branches, fixed product IDs, product prompts and installer policy. Rejected.

## B. Port verified capabilities into the existing CIC workflow
Preserves dynamic product ranking and organization scope while adopting continuity, evidence, safety and handoff controls. Selected.

Rollback: re-import the pre-change raw export kept only in protected temporary storage during implementation, or restore sanitized Git baseline and rebind credentials.
